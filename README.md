# Zirui Li · 李梓睿

This repository hosts my personal homepage: [j1ml1.github.io](https://j1ml1.github.io).

Hi, I'm **Zirui Li (李梓睿)**, a second-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, supervised by [Prof. Xuefeng Bai](https://goodbai-nlp.github.io/) and [Prof. Kehai Chen](https://chenkehai.github.io/). My current research interests include LLM reasoning, interpretability, and Latent Chain-of-Thought.

Before starting my Ph.D., I was a Master's student at the University of Manchester, working with [Yizhi Li](https://yizhilll.github.io/) and [Siwei Wu](https://wusiwei0410.github.io/) in [Prof. Chenghua Lin](https://chenghua-lin.github.io/)'s lab, where I focused on multimodal retrieval. I received my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from the University of Manchester, supervised by [Prof. Rizos Sakellariou](https://www.cs.man.ac.uk/~rizos/), and during that time I was interested in social network analysis.
